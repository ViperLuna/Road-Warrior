10.14.2026
  Building Patience - Now ON. A house with no road to a destination of its colour, a destination no house of its colours can reach, the stadium, and the side roads all get a countdown: 60 seconds of quiet, then a warning toast, a pulsing red ring and 60 more seconds. If it still isn't connected when that runs out, the game is over. Connect it in time and the clock resets. If you break a connection later, the clock starts over. Pausing stops every clock, so you can pause and build.

  Accidents (after 1,000 trips) - Now and then two cars bump and block their lane for 20 seconds while the police show up. Cars behind them still get angry.
  Multicolor destinations now start after 2,000 trips (was 3,000).
  Stadium (after 3,000 trips, Highlands and Metro) - A 4x3 stadium is donated to the city. Cars of any colour can go there, and it has two gates. It must be connected to houses of every colour (at least one of each to start). If there is no room for it, it clears a 5x4 area, roads first and buildings only if it has to.
  Side Roads (after 4,000 trips) - Two roads to other cities appear on two edges of the map. They can't be demolished. Each must connect to the stadium and to destinations of every colour. Once connected, cars of any colour arrive from outside to visit the stadium, more and more often as the minutes go by. Every 5 to 10 minutes a game is hosted at the stadium and a horde of them shows up at once, a little bigger each time.
  A popup explains the stadium and the side roads and the clock only starts when you tap OK.

  A road with a blinking, jammed car on it can't be demolished until the jam clears.
  History Log - New "Log" button. It lists the toasts (new buildings, accidents, traffic jams, the stadium, side roads, patience warnings). Tap one and the camera flies to the place and highlights it. Accidents remember where they were, even after they clear.
  Countdown chips under the top bar show how long the stadium or the side roads have left. Tap one to fly there.

  New map: Metro (100 x 66), unlocked with 150 trips on Highlands.
  Sound effects and music - 13 songs, random order, volume and mute for each in Settings. Number keys and the numpad work as the buttons.
  Your best score updates live now, so a run that never jams still counts.

10.12.2026
  New Destination Types: Single Unit and Multicolor
  Single Unit - One tile building with 2 parking spaces. This allows more destinations when spaces start getting tight.
  Multicolor - Two different colors access the same destination. These spawn in after 3,000 trips have been completed.

  Parking Lot exits are no longer limited to the front entrance. They can be toggled to any available side.

  End Game Option - You can end your game at any time and record your trip count. Ending the game is equivalent to losing to gridlock or impatience.

  Construction Cones Removed
  Cones were initially meant to block traffic while working on a road to stop cars from getting stuck in a parking lot after reaching their destination. This is no longer an issue because cars automatically build ghost roads to ensure they return back to their homes if a route back home is no longer available. The cone will remain in the game, however, for a possible future difficulty setting.

10.10.2026
  Game Saves - Now implemented. Currently game saves are local browser storage. The game automatically saves every 10 seconds, when switching tabs, switching apps, locking the phone, or when you tap "Menu."
  Speed Control - Paused, 1x, 1.5x, 2x. Game loads paused by default.
  Construction Zones - Choosing the construction tool and tapping/dragging a road will drop cones down and prevent traffic from entering while you fix or build new roads that may intersect with active traffic. Cars that have already planned to use that road will still utilize it until they finish their full route and return home.
  Overpasses work with either road size. While the Overpass button is toggled on, tapping the overpass tile will alternate which road becomes the overpass.
  Bridges can intersect and can also be cut from intersection unless they are perpendicular to each other. Two bridges crossing cannot be cut.
  Traffic is even smoother now with much better roundabout control. Cars can turn right on red. 

10.09.2026
  Intersection Cuts - Roads adjacent to other roads can be cut from each other to remove unwanted intersections. Use the cut tool and swipe the branch between tiles to remove it. Swiping again will replace it. You can toggle cut views in the settings. Cuts are always visible while using the cut tool.
  Destination building parking lots can be flipped to change which side of the lot exits to the road. Tap the building to rotate it and tap the parking lot to flip the exits.
  Traffic jams is still a difficult work in progress.
  Traffic is smoother with less unnecessary gridlock. A car that would not otherwise be locked will force itself forward to reset its impatience timer. They may phase through other cars if the game notices traffic is stopped with nothing blocking it.
