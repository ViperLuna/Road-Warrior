10.14.2026
  Building Patience - Now ON. A house with no road to a destination of its color, a destination no house of its colors can reach, the stadium, and the side roads all get a countdown: 60 seconds of quiet, then a warning toast, a pulsing red ring and 60 more seconds. If it still isn't connected when that runs out, the game is over. Connect it in time and the clock resets. If you break a connection later, the clock starts over. Pausing stops every clock, so you can pause and build.

  Accidents (after 1,000 trips) - Now and then two cars bump and block their lane for 20 seconds while the police show up. Cars behind them still get angry.
  Multicolor destinations now start after 2,000 trips (was 3,000).
  Stadium (after 3,000 trips, Highlands and Metro) - A 4x3 stadium is donated to the city. Cars of any color can go there, and it has two gates. It must be connected to houses of every color (at least one of each to start). If there is no room for it, it clears a 5x4 area, roads first and buildings only if it has to.
  Side Roads (after 4,000 trips) - Two roads to other cities appear on two edges of the map. At 5,000 trips the other two edges get theirs. They can't be demolished. Each must connect to the stadium and to destinations of every color. Once connected, cars of any color arrive from outside to visit the stadium, more and more often as the minutes go by. Every 5 to 10 minutes a game is hosted at the stadium and a horde of them shows up at once, a little bigger each time.
  A popup explains the stadium and the side roads and the clock only starts when you tap OK.

  A road with a blinking, jammed car on it can't be demolished until the jam clears.
  History Log - New "Log" button. It lists the toasts (new buildings, accidents, traffic jams, the stadium, side roads, patience warnings). Tap one and the camera flies to the place and highlights it. Accidents remember where they were, even after they clear.
  Countdown chips under the top bar show how long the stadium or the side roads have left. Tap one to fly there.

  New map: Metro (100 x 66), unlocked with 150 trips on Highlands.
  Sound effects and music - 13 songs, random order, volume and mute for each in Settings. Number keys and the numpad work as the buttons.
  Your best score updates live now, so a run that never jams still counts.
  Hotkeys can be changed: Settings > Keyboard. Every action has up to two keys (click a box and press a key, or two keys together); a taken key asks if you want to swap; Reset puts the defaults back. New defaults: S Shop, L Log, G Bridge, T Tunnel, I Traffic light, N Music, V Overpass, H Highway, O Roundabout (those need the button to be showing). One-way stays on the 5 key.
  Single units now turn on their own axis: tapping one swings its parking lot clockwise around the building to the next free side (it only needs that one tile). If every side is blocked it flips in place instead. The stadium can also be flipped half way round in place when there is no room for a quarter turn.
  Tunnels are dug one block at a time now. Each block uses one tunnel piece ($60 in the Shop, or a reward gives a few). Drag from a road into a hill and keep going; tunnels can bend and branch, can be cut like roads, and can be demolished a block at a time. A half-dug tunnel is just a road that goes nowhere yet. Anyone who already had tunnels gets 4 free pieces for each one, for testing early.
  Filling the map with road to stop the game from growing no longer works: the city clears a patch of road to make room for new buildings, and the side roads and the stadium always find a spot (clearing road first, knocking down a building only as a last resort). A wave of side roads that could not place all of its gates keeps trying for the rest.


- Houses and destinations now keep two tiles away from the edge of the map, so the side-road gates always have room. They only go there if nothing else fits.

- Traffic lights now wait for a real gap: the green only gives way after its street has been empty for 2 seconds (a one-car hole in a stream no longer flips it), or at the 10 second cap whatever is still coming. Both streets follow the same rules.
- Accidents now last 40 seconds. A car that stops behind one can't reach the 40 second game-over time before it clears, so the queue just gets to move again.

- A jam now also locks the road tile ahead of the stuck car: you can't demolish the tile under it or the one it is waiting to enter, so you can't clear a jam by deleting the intersection it is waiting for. Roads beside it are still free.

- Road works: from 1,500 trips, the accident timer sometimes brings road works instead of a crash. One straight road tile gets little orange cones down one lane. The cars on that side wait for the oncoming traffic to clear, then swing into the open lane, so the tile works like a one-lane bridge. It lasts 40 seconds, can land on either side of the road, and never puts a wreck and road works on the map together.

- The road under a wreck, and the tile with road works, can't be demolished until the police or the crew are done.

- Fixed: cars coming out of a stadium gate started from the left-hand bay and swung across the whole gate, taking up both lanes and holding up traffic. Now cars go in and out of a stadium gate in a straight line in their own lane, and a car leaving no longer blocks one arriving.

- Fixed: on 4-lane roads (highways) cars picked the inner or outer lane at random, so half of them swerved across the road to make a turn, most visibly at a stadium gate. Now a car takes the lane its turn needs: outer lane for right turns, inner lane for left turns, and it moves over early instead of at the last moment. A car coming out of a gate turns into the nearest lane.

- Traffic lights can now go on a bridge, on the tile where a road meets the span from the side (a plain stretch of bridge still has no intersection to light). Roundabouts and overpasses still can't go on a bridge, and a light on a bridge comes back to your stock if you demolish the bridge.

- Cars from the side roads no longer only head for the stadium. A visitor now goes to any destination it can reach that takes its color, the stadium included (the stadium takes every color). A match crowd still goes to the stadium only.

- Some houses now get a second car (10% of them, set in config/tuning.json as houses.secondCarChance). The two cars make their own trips and each trip counts, so a busy map gets busier. The second one waits for the first to pull out of the driveway.

- The match at the stadium now waits for the crowd: once the last car of a crowd has set off, there is a 30 second grace period. If everyone has parked before it runs out, the game starts right away; if some are still on the road when it runs out, the game starts anyway and the stragglers wait inside when they arrive.

- A saved game now opens in Move mode, so a stray tap can't build or demolish anything while you find your bearings. A new game starts in Build mode.

- Oncoming cars that both turn left at the same intersection no longer make one wait for the other. Left and right turns now cut in a little earlier (tighter curves), so the two left turns pass each other in the middle like real drivers do, and the paths of other turns that don't really cross stay clear of each other.

- Fixed: on a 4-lane road, two cars side by side at a green light could hold each other up. If the straight-on car in one lane couldn't move because the road beyond was backed up, a car in the next lane that wanted to turn waited for it too, even though its own way was clear. A car that can't move off the line no longer holds anyone else up. In bot test games this cut the number of jam warnings roughly in half.

- Fixed: on a 4-lane road, a car turning left from the inner lane waited until every straight-on car in the outer lane had cleared the intersection, even though their paths don't cross. Cars in the two lanes of the same approach now only wait for each other if their paths really cross (for example a car changing lanes). Streets that cross keep the wider safety margin.

- New houses now pick their color completely at random (one in however many colors are in play), so one color can't pile up houses by collecting destinations. There is a new Difficulty section in Settings with a "Color spawn demographics" checkbox: tick it to bring back the old balancing, where the color with the fewest houses per destination gets the next house. The choice is locked in when a game starts, and saved games that were started before this change now pick at random too.
- The game now uses American English everywhere (color, center, neighbor).

- There is a new eye button in the bottom right corner. Tap it (or press U) to hide every button, counter and message and see the bare map, for example for a screenshot. Tap it again to bring them back. It is not saved, so a reload starts with them showing.
